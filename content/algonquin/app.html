Before you paddle

This is a planning tool only.

Routes, portages and access points are approximations from OpenStreetMap and official data. Conditions change — low water, closures, dams and portage reroutes happen every season.

Check conditions with the park before going on a trip: Algonquin Park advisories and the official park map.

About this planner

About & credits

Pick any named lake, portage landing or official access point in Algonquin Provincial Park and get a paddling route with every portage listed in order: how long it is, how far it climbs, and an elevation profile for the trail. Four ways to weight the answer:

  • Balanced — portages and the lakes you have to cross
  • Easiest portages — steers around the steep ones
  • Fewest portages — the shortest portage chain, whatever it costs
  • Least total portaging — the shortest walks on the portages

Built from OpenStreetMap — over 7,000 water bodies, more than 600 portages and 3,200 river reaches, plus all 29 official access points — and refreshed as the map changes.

Created by Yu Chen Hou. Open source at github.com/yucombinator/trailstolove.

Not a substitute for the official park map. Check current conditions with Algonquin Provincial Park before you go.
