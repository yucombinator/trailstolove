# Algonquin Provincial Park — published canoe-route reference

Working reference for validating the route planner at https://hikewithyu.com/algonquin/ against
what paddlers actually report doing. Compiled 2026-10-04.

Rules applied: place names are reproduced **as the source writes them**. No number appears here
unless it is stated in the cited source.

Note on `Type`: `trip report` = a paddler describing a trip they did. `guidebook listing` = an
outfitter, park page or route-database describing what a route *is*.

## Reported pain points

Route *properties*, not conditions.

1. **Map length != ground length on long portages.** Bonfield-Dickson is "P5470" on the map, but
   took "about an hour and a half... for 5.5 kilometers", with 4 km of portaging already done
   mid-carry. https://allofalgonquin.com/2022/09/22/crow-river-loopish-proulx-to-dickson-and-everything-in-between/

2. **Hogan -> Big Crow officially 3,750 m, field-measured ~4,700 m.** "Field reports consistently
   suggest it is closer to 4,700 metres. The first 400 to 500 metres climb steeply uphill."
   Overgrown boardwalk where overhead branches catch carried canoes.
   https://borealbase.ca/trips/routes/ontario/opeongo-cedar-loop

3. **The 805 m Maple Creek portage is called one of the most difficult in the park.** "It sucks.
   It really, truly sucks. It's only 805 meters but it feels like every damn meter is higher than
   the one that came before." Maple Lake is 75 m above Kioshkokwi over six portages, with no canoe
   elevators installed.
   https://allofalgonquin.com/2020/07/14/return-to-kiosk-pt-1-bright-days-bigger-bugs-biggar-lake/

4. **Maple Creek is the "Boat Destroyer"** - "a shallow rocky descent with multiple beaver dams and
   difficult portage landings. Plan for canoe scrapes."
   https://borealbase.ca/trips/routes/ontario/algonquin-north

5. **A portage ending in a swamp with floating logs (Little Crooked, P1100).** "The last 50 meters
   leading into Little Crooked is swamp... most of those logs were free floating."
   https://allofalgonquin.com/2022/09/22/crow-river-loopish-proulx-to-dickson-and-everything-in-between/

6. **The "Surprise Portage" on Cedar->Radiant is badly conditioned despite a flat-looking profile.**
   "Just because the highest point is at one end... doesn't mean there can't be a crap load of up
   and down afterwards."
   https://allofalgonquin.com/2017/10/02/radiant-lake/

7. **Two near-identically named portages on one lake.** At Merchant: "I had taken us to the
   Merchant/Big Trout portage instead of the Merchant/Blowdown one."
   https://allofalgonquin.com/2018/09/23/into-the-wind-merchant-lake-and-more/

8. **Two lakes connected by marsh, not a portage.** Rattrap to Dahinda: "There's no portage between
   Rattrap and Dahinda... Instead, you paddle through a short marshy bit."
   https://allofalgonquin.com/2020/07/14/return-to-kiosk-pt-1-bright-days-bigger-bugs-biggar-lake/

9. **A portage crossing a paved highway.** Smoke->Ouse (1390 m) crosses Highway 60 - the paddler
   stood in the road with the canoe on his shoulders.
   https://allofalgonquin.com/2016/06/28/canoe-to-canisbay/

10. **Route length depends on which version of a portage you take.** Proulx to Opeongo is "either a
    P175 and a P965 broken up by a paddle across a small pond, or an uninterrupted P1395."
    https://allofalgonquin.com/2022/09/22/crow-river-loopish-proulx-to-dickson-and-everything-in-between/

11. **Signed portage length disagrees with the map.** Canoe->Joe dam carry: "We've used Jeff's Map's
    240 m here but note that the sign at the landing says 295 m." It is the busiest portage in the
    park - the "highway portage."
    https://betweenlakes.ca/trips-routes/tepee-lake-canoe-route-algonquin/

12. **A river drawn as a short paddle but is not.** Little Madawaska between Radiant and Hogan: "it
    appears as a simple two-kilometre paddle... In reality the river braids through extensive beaver
    meadows, and the twists and turns can easily double the actual paddling distance... the standard
    Algonquin canoe routes map is nearly useless for this section."
    https://borealbase.ca/trips/routes/ontario/opeongo-cedar-loop

13. **Crow River oxbows roughly double the mapped distance.** "Although the map shows roughly five
    kilometres between the lakes, the actual paddling distance through the river's bends is closer
    to ten." https://borealbase.ca/trips/routes/ontario/opeongo-cedar-loop

14. **Crow River carries seven portages in its upper reach and looks paddleable when it isn't.**
    "There are 7 portages along the Crow River between Big Crow and Crow Bay and... a couple of
    spots where the water levels are just high enough to make it look like you might be able to
    paddle through, even though you most certainly cannot."
    https://allofalgonquin.com/2022/09/22/crow-river-loopish-proulx-to-dickson-and-everything-in-between/

15. **A lake shallow enough to require dragging the last third.** Radiant: "You can see bottom a
    long way from shore... I've heard stories of people having to drag their canoes along the
    bottom for the final third of the lake." https://allofalgonquin.com/2017/10/02/radiant-lake/

16. **A water-level-dependent corridor that is neither portage nor river.** Lost Joe between Baby
    Joe and Little Joe: "a shallow, rocky river corridor that is generally considered a soft swift
    rather than a true portage... in late summer low water you may be dragging over rocks, while in
    high water the current can make sections tricky." Up to +430 m of portaging if you can't run it.
    https://betweenlakes.ca/trips-routes/canoe-lake-loop-algonquin-park/

17. **The Amable du Fond routinely runs too low to paddle.** "The first half of which is a winding
    creek that would be really frustrating with low water levels... we lifted over and spent the
    next half hour poling along the creek."
    https://allofalgonquin.com/2016/06/22/north-tea-lake/

18. **A portage with a pond in the middle of it.** North Tea -> Lost Dog: "a small pond in the middle
    of that portage that you have to paddle across and which is... swarming with leeches."
    https://allofalgonquin.com/2016/06/22/north-tea-lake/

19. **A flooded-out portage described as "a bog walk of sadness".** Sunbeam -> Canada Jay: "Right now
    it's flooded in a couple of places, and it's muddy AF in many, many others."
    https://allofalgonquin.com/2017/08/23/the-brent-crawl-part-1-white-trout-or-bust/

20. **A creek on the map that is not a route.** At Biggar: "Birchcliffe Creek enters from the south,
    but paddlers hoping to fish the creek will find it badly overgrown and largely impassable by
    canoe. The creek is not a practical route toward Birchcliffe Lake or Raven Lake."
    https://borealbase.ca/trips/routes/ontario/north-tea-biggar-three-mile-manitou-loop

21. **The Merchant Lake portage landing blocks water access.** At the 340 m Happy Isle->Merchant
